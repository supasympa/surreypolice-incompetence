Exhibit 01 — Child Safeguarding

The children the system missed

Referrals that went nowhere. Concerns raised, logged, and then — silence. The documented record shows what happened when Surrey Police received child protection referrals and failed to act on them in time, or at all.

This is not allegation. It is the force's own paperwork, obtained through FOI and subject access requests, read alongside the findings of His Majesty's Inspectorate of Constabulary and Fire & Rescue Services. The gap between what was referred and what was done is the story.

For the families involved, the gap was measured in months. For the children, it was measured in harm that a functioning safeguarding system is supposed to prevent.
