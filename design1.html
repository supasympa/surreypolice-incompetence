Questions

Frequently asked.

Is this legal?

Yes. Publication is protected under Article 10 ECHR, and every document cited is one we are lawfully entitled to publish.

Are the documents real?

Yes — FOI responses, HMICFRS reports, IOPC correspondence and court records, published in full.

What is RUI?

Released Under Investigation: pre-charge bail's replacement. No conditions, no supervision, often no end date.

Can I help?

Yes. Send your documents through the confidential channel, or tell the press desk this story matters.

Will you take it down if pressured?

Only a court can decide that. Pressure is not a legal argument.
