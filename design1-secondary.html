PUBLIC DISCLOSURE — SURREY POLICE

The children the system missed.

A documented account of how Surrey Police handled child protection referrals — and what the inspection record says went wrong.

Article · 01

The safeguarding record.

The documented record shows referrals that went nowhere. Child protection concerns were raised, logged, and then allowed to drift — no recorded action, no follow-up, no closure. What the HMICFRS inspection findings describe is not a single oversight but a pattern: a force that received information about children at risk and could not demonstrate it acted on it.

This is not anecdote. It is what the inspection reports say, what the FOI responses confirm, and what the complaint trail could not answer. The children the system missed are not names in this record — they are absences, gaps where action should have been recorded and was not.

Surrey Police's own correspondence, published here in full, acknowledges the shortfall. What it does not do is explain why, for eighteen months, a person could be left under investigation with no conditions, no review and no protection — while referrals about children sat in the same system, unactioned.

"The absence of recorded action is itself a record."
— From the case file
