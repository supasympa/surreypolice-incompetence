How to choose

  1. Open all four homepages — they work on your phone too.
  2. Compare them side by side; imagine your name and photos in each.
  3. Reply to our email with the design number (1–4).

Nothing is final: colours, fonts and layout details can all be tuned once you've picked a direction. If you like two, tell us — we can blend elements.
