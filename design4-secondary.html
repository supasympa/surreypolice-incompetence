SURREY POLICE — SAFEGUARDING RECORD

The children the system missed

A documented account of child protection referrals that went nowhere — drawn from HMICFRS inspection findings, FOI responses and the complaint trail.

001
GRADE: REQUIRES IMPROVEMENT

The safeguarding record is the first entry in this ledger because it is where the documented failures begin. A referral was made. The record shows no action taken. No follow-up recorded. No outcome logged. The child protection system, as it operated here, produced a gap — and the gap is visible only because the paperwork exists to show it.

HMICFRS inspection findings, published in January 2025, identified weaknesses in how Surrey Police handled child protection concerns. The findings were not abstract: they pointed to specific failures in referral handling, in risk assessment, and in the gap between a concern being raised and a response being recorded.

This page sets out what the documents show — not what is alleged, not what is suspected, but what the inspection body recorded and what the force’s own paperwork confirms.
